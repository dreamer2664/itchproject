GRIMFORGE — how to run the forge

Goal: fulfil trade contracts. Goods only count once they are belted into the Trade Post (the flagged cave mouth), then press Deliver on the contract card.

1-6 pick building · R rotate ghost · left click place · right click demolish (half refund) · middle drag or arrow keys pan · wheel zoom · space pause · F cycle speed · H this help.

Chains: miner on a glowing vein → belt → smelter (2 ore + 1 coal = 1 ingot) → belt → assembler (recipes on its card) → belt → trade post. Machines run half speed without a goblin operator standing at them (crew need farms for food and beds to sleep).

Same seed = same cave, same veins, same contract book.

One self-contained file. No uploads, no accounts, no neural content — every pixel and every goblin is math.
